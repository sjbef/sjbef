# SJBEF Scholarship Gallery Photos

This directory is the dedicated home for photographs related to SJBEF scholarship events, recipient presentation nights, and chapter dinners.

## How to Add Photos to the Gallery

1. **Upload**: Drop your photo (JPEG or PNG format, under 2MB recommended for fast loading) directly into this folder.
2. **Name your photo**: Use clean lowercase naming with underscores (e.g. `2024_scholarship_recipient_name.jpg`).
3. **Reference your photo**:
   - Files here are served directly. A photo at `/public/images/gallery/scholarships/my_photo.jpg` is referenced in the app as `/images/gallery/scholarships/my_photo.jpg`.
4. **Publishing**:
   - You can add photos to the live gallery dynamically through the **Admin Panel** on the website, or by editing the `defaultGalleryItems` array in the code.
