# SJBEF Grant Gallery Photos

This directory is the dedicated home for photographs related to SJBEF Catholic school grants, classroom presentations, bilingual library materials, and French studies programs.

## How to Add Photos to the Gallery

1. **Upload**: Place your photo (JPEG or PNG, under 2MB recommended) in this directory.
2. **Naming**: Use clean lowercase naming with underscores (e.g., `2024_holy_ghost_grant.jpg`).
3. **Reference**:
   - Files here are served directly. A photo at `/public/images/gallery/grants/my_photo.jpg` is referenced in the app as `/images/gallery/grants/my_photo.jpg`.
4. **Publishing**:
   - You can add photos to the live gallery dynamically through the **Admin Panel** on the website, or by editing the `defaultGalleryItems` array in the code.
